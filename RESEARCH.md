# Research Notes

## AI-Generated Assets

The following visual assets on the portfolio were generated with AI tools:

- Homepage workspace illustration — generated with Gemini and used on the Home page.
- Cybersecurity network illustration — generated with Gemini and used on the Home page.
- Portfolio project cards — generated with Copilot and used on the Portfolio page.
- Link card thumbnails — generated with Copilot and used on the Links page.

These assets were selected to support the portfolio theme and provide a polished visual identity while keeping the content accessible and consistent.

## Implementation Notes

- The site uses React + Vite with client-side routing and Supabase for the contact form and admin authentication.
- The contact form includes client-side validation and clear feedback states for success and error handling.
- The layout was designed to remain responsive and readable in both light and dark themes.
