## Perspective 2

Damien!!! This is fantastic! I only have a few small feedback items:

1. I loved the way you tied in your military experience with coding and cybersecurity. I'm inclined to say I want more of that. The line that talks about "mission success" is fantastic, and I'd love for the military/cybersecurity tie-in to be woven throughout the pitch using similar language.
2. Small public speaking polishing points: try to incorporate some hand movements in there. Right now it feels a little rigid and stiff, so some hand movements and tone inflections will make you come across more comfortable in this position of "selling" yourself.
3. Small note: in this type of recording, I think long pauses tend to come across a little awkward. Pauses make sense when you're speaking to another person, but in a video, I would just flow to the next sentence.

These are small notes, but overall a really strong pitch!
